Quick Reference & Workflow Guide

How VaultSync Works

A streamlined guide to managing your family budget, protected buckets, and AI tools.

1

Set Monthly Budget

Click Month Budget on the Overview page to set your family's monthly income and spending target.

2

Schedule Bills & Buckets

Add fixed bills (Rent, EMI) or Protected Buckets (Groceries, Fuel) that automatically draw down as you spend.

3

Log or Scan Expenses

Record purchases manually, scan a receipt photo/PDF, import a full bank statement, or tell the AI Assistant.

4

Review AI Insights

Check Financial Analytics for category variance and Daily AI Briefings for safe daily spend limits.

Overview & Daily Ledger

Open Overview

The Overview gives you a real-time pulse on your monthly budget, daily burn rate, category doughnut chart, and planned commitments. The Daily Ledger focuses on a single date with a 1-click toggle between All Family and Only Mine.

Protected Buckets vs. Planned Bills

Manage Plan

Planned Bills are one-off monthly payments (like Rent or Broadband) that you mark Paid when posted. Protected Buckets reserve a chunk of budget for a category (like Groceries) and automatically subtract daily expenses logged in that category.

Financial Analytics & Daily AI Briefings

Open Reports

Compare your 6-month cash flow, household P&L, and category variance against both your own plan and the AI benchmark. Every night at 11:58 PM, VaultSync also prepares a Daily AI Briefing with tomorrow's safe spend target.

AI Budget Planner & Assistant

Open AI Planner

Generate a complete monthly budget plan in one click, preview category deltas side-by-side, apply it to your schedule, and revert anytime from the Rollback History.
