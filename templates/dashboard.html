VaultSync Feature Guide

Everything you need to know about what does what

Dashboard & Ledger (Home)

Your central command center. See your 4 core numbers (Budget, Spent, Planned Bills, Projected Savings), quickly log a daily expense or scan a receipt, view category shares, and browse the complete full-width ledger with 1-click spender filters.

Daily Diary

Groups purchases day-by-day in chronological order (Today, Yesterday, Oct 08, etc.) like a passbook or diary. Switch between All Family and Only Mine to audit spending on specific dates.

My Spendings

Shows only the expenses you personally paid for. It strips away expenses logged by other family members so you can manage your personal spending and category totals independently.

Financial Analytics

Detailed reporting showing 6-month historical cash flow trends, weekly burn rate, category spending variance against limits, and top spending days of the month.

Daily AI Briefings

Every day, AI calculates your safe daily spending limit for the remaining days of the month, highlights any abnormal surges, and gives you actionable recommendations to prevent overspending.

AI Budget Studio

Simulate and generate smart monthly budget targets based on your family's actual past spending patterns. Compare deltas, apply the plan in 1 click, or revert to past versions anytime.

AI Financial Advisor & Decision Studio

Analyze deep spending patterns across Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual timeframes. Uncovers leakages, provides tailored tips, and asks structured Antigravity decision questions to calibrate a custom family savings plan.

Download Excel Template & Auto-Capture

Download our official pre-formatted Excel expense sheet with automated formulas every month. When ready, upload the sheet, CSV, or a bank PDF/photo into Import Book to auto-sync transactions and budgets instantly!

Family Admin & Data Reset

Manage family members, generate invite codes, set category monthly caps, and safely erase all financial test data whenever you want a fresh monthly start.
